# Who is She?

The wife of J Robert Oppenheimer and a former Communist Party USA member who struggles with marital issues.
## Relationships with Other Characters

1. [[movie4character1.md|J Robert Oppenheimer]] - Supportive of him yet embittered due to his infidelity and secrecy.
2. [[movie4character2.md|Lewis Strauss]] - Indirectly affected due to his vendetta against her husband.
### What Happens to Her at the End of the Movie?

Kitty remains loyal to her husband, standing by and defending him during the security clearance hearings.
#### Famous Quote

> "You think because you let them tar and feather you that the world will forgive you? It won't."
