# Who is He?

J Robert Oppenheimer is a theoretical physicist who was the head of the Manhattan Project that developed the first nuclear weapons.
## Relationships with Other Characters

1. [[movie4character2.md|Lewis Strauss]] - He naively trusts Strauss and is unaware of his growing resentment against him until it is too late.
2. [[movie4character3.md|Kitty Oppenheimer]] - His wife whose marriage becomes strained due to infidelity, secrecy, and emotional detachment due to his work, but they remain together.
### What Happens to Him at the End of the Movie?

His security clearance is revoked in 1954 after Strauss secretly organizes a humiliating hearing. He accepts the disgrace as a form of atonement for his sin of bringing nuclear weapons to the world.
#### Famous Quote

> "Now I am become death, the destroyer of worlds."
