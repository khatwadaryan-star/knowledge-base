# Who is He?

The Chairman of the Atomic Energy Commission who grows resentful of J Robert Oppenheimer and becomes his secret antagonist.
## Relationships with Other Characters

1. [[movie4character1.md|J Robert Oppenheimer]] - He resents him for a perceived hatred against him and policy disagreements, particularly their views on the hydrogen bomb.
2. [[movie4character3.md|Kitty Oppenheimer]] - While there is no direct relationship, his actions have a profound impact on her due to her association with her husband.
### What Happens to Him at the End of the Movie?

Strauss succeeds in revoking J Robert Oppenheimer's security clearance, but later faces his own humiliation when his motives are revealed during Senate hearings, costing him his political career.
#### Famous Quote

> "Amateurs seek the sun. Get eaten. Power stays in the shadows."
