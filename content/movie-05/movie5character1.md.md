# Who is He?

Leonard is a former insurance investigator who suffers from anterograde amnesia, which means he is unable to form long-term memories. As such, he relies on Polaroid photos, notes, and tattoos in order to get revenge on the man responsible for his condition and his wife's murder.
## Relationships with Other Characters

1. [[movie5character2.md|Natalie]]- He is manipulated Natalie as a tool to target her own enemies.
2. [[movie5character3.md|Teddy]] - He initially distrusts Teddy but relies on him for guidance in his quest for vengeance.
### What Happens to Him at the End of the Movie?

Leonard realizes that he has already killed the man responsible, but chooses to deceive himself by planting false clues to paint Teddy as the killer in order to get justice against anyone who wronged him (Teddy used Leonard's condition for his own benefit).
#### Famous Quote

> "We all lie to ourselves to be happy."
