# Who is He?

Teddy is a corrupt undercover cop who initially helped Leonard avenge his wife but later exploited him to kill criminals for profit.
## Relationships with Other Characters

1. [[movie5character1.md|Leonard]] - He acts as a friend who secretly uses Leonard's condition for his own benefit.
2. [[movie5character2.md|Natalie]] - Their interests clash in regards to Jimmy's apparent disappearance and Dodd's fate.
### What Happens to Him at the End of the Movie?

Teddy is killed by Leonard as he is deliberately set up by Leonard to be the murderer as a way to get revenge on Teddy for using him.

> "You don't want the truth. You make up your own truth."
