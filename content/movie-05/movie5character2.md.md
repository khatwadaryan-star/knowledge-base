# Who is She?

Natalie is a bartender whose boyfriend, Jimmy Grantz, was killed by Leonard, unbeknownst to her. She subsequently exploits Leonard's condition to eliminate threats in her life like Dodd.
## Relationships with Other Characters

1. [[movie5character1.md|Leonard]] - She uses his memory loss to her advantage, while offering him temporary companionship.
2. [[movie5character3.md|Teddy]] - She distrusts Teddy, seeing him as corrupt and self-serving.
### What Happens to Her at the End of the Movie?

Natalie survives the events of the film, having successfully gotten what she wanted out of Leonard's condition. However, her manipulations leave Leonard further entangled in a web of lies.
