# Who is She?

Mal is Cobb's late wife and partner during dream exploration. After spending decades in a shared dream world, she lost her grip on reality, as she still believed the real world was a dream, which later resulted in her suicide. She appears as a projection of Cobb's subconscious during the inception mission.
## Relationships with Other Characters

1. [[movie3character1.md|Cobb]] - Her husband, whom she has genuine love for, but her descent into delusion creates conflict and results in tragic suicide. In dreams, she becomes his biggest obstacle in the inception mission by reminding him of his trauma.
### What Happens to Her at the End of the Movie?

Mal's projection is confronted by Cobb, who rejects her by accepting her death, allowing him to successfully complete the inception mission and reunite with their children.
