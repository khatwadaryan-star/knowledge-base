# Who is He?

Cobb is a dream extractor who specializes in stealing secrets from people's subconscious during dreams. He accepts a dangerous mission of inception (planting an idea in someone's mind) in order to reunite with his children.
## Relationships with Other Characters

1. [[movie3character2.md|Mal]] - His deceased wife who is the manifestation of his guilt within the dream world, as her death was influenced by his manipulation of her perception of reality.
### What Happens to Him at the End of the Movie?

Cobb successfully completes the inception mission by letting go of his attachment to Mal, allowing him to reunite with his children.
#### Famous Quote

> "An idea is like a virus, resilient, highly contagious. The smallest seed of an idea can grow. It can grow to define or destroy you."
