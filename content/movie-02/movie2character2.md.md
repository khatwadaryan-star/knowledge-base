# Who is She?

Murph is a curious child who grows up into a brilliant NASA scientist driven by both resentment of her father's departure and hope in his promise.
## Relationships with Other Characters

1. [[movie2character1.md|Cooper]] - Her father whom she idolizes but feels betrayed when he leaves. This motivates her to enter science. Ultimately, she realizes Cooper was helping her through time and she forgives him.
### What Happens to Her at the End of the Movie?

Murph deciphers the quantum data from Cooper across time, solves the equation of gravity, and enables humanity's escape from the dying Earth. She lives to old age on the new home and reconciles with Cooper when he returns.
#### Famous Quote

> "Nobody believed me, but I knew you'd come back."
