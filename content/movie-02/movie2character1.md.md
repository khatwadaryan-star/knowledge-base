# Who is He?

Cooper is a former NASA pilot turned farmer in a dying Earth plagued by crop blight and dust storms. He becomes recruited for a secret mission to find a new habitat for humanity.
## Relationships with Other Characters

1. [[movie2character2.md|Murph]] - His daughter whom his promise of returning home to drives him throughout the film.
### What Happens to Him at the End of the Movie?

Cooper enters a black hole and is able to transmit quantum data from inside to Murph in the past, allowing her to solve the gravitational problem of mass migration from Earth. He is later rescued by humanity's new home and reunites with an older Murph due to time dilation.
#### Famous Quote

> "Mankind was born on Earth. It was never meant to die here."
